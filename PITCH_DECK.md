---

### SLIDE 2 — THE HOOK (the problem)

Every god-game and life-sim makes the same promise — *"a living world"* — and breaks it the same way:

- **Sims** worlds are tiny and freeze when you look away.
- **Civ** is brilliant but it's a spreadsheet wearing a map.
- **Spore** had scale but no soul once you zoomed in.

> **Nobody has built a planet where every being actually thinks — and keeps living whether you're watching or not.**

That's the gap. Modern AI agents finally make it buildable.

---

### SLIDE 3 — THE GAME (one sentence)

> **Shape a primordial Earth, seed it with sentient AI races, and let go.**

Every being is an autonomous agent with memory, personality, and ambition. Civilizations rise, split, war, trade, and invent things you never planned — **forever, and uniquely, in every player's world.**

---

### SLIDE 4 — THREE FANTASIES, ONE SEAMLESS WORLD

The whole game is the ability to drop between scales with **no loading screen**:

| | You are… | View | The feeling |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🌍 **God** | overseer of the planet | orbital → continental | *awe* |
| 🏘️ **Watcher** | observer of one family | street-level, cinematic | *intimacy* (Sims 4) |
| 👤 **Possessed** | a guiding spirit inside ONE being | first/third person | *immersion* (GTA VI) |

Orbit Pangaea → swoop into a Stone-Age village → pick one hunter → **live a day as his guardian spirit** → let go → fast-forward 800 years → his descendants worship a "Sky Watcher." **That's you.**

---

### SLIDE 5 — THE MAGIC: AUTONOMOUS BEINGS

Not NPCs. **Agents.** Each has:

- **Needs** (hunger, rest, safety, social, purpose) — Sims-style drives
- **Personality** — brave/timid, kind/cruel, devout/skeptical…
- **Memory** — who helped them, who wronged them, what they saw
- **Beliefs** — including their theory of *you*, the god
- **A life arc** — born → elder → death; **lineages** persist

Religions, languages, governments, art, and inventions **emerge**. Nothing is scripted. **We don't know what your world will do. Neither will you.**

---

### SLIDE 6 — THE TECH THAT MAKES IT POSSIBLE

**Cognitive Level-of-Detail** — our core innovation.

- Graphics LOD reduces distant geometry. **We reduce distant *thought*.**
- 100,000+ beings tick as cheap utility-AI.
- The few hundred near you (or pivotal to a story) get promoted to full **LLM "souls"** — real dialogue, planning, scheming, invention.
- Promote/demote dynamically as the camera and the story move.

**Result:** a planet that's affordable to run *and* alive up close. This is the moat.

---

### SLIDE 7 — A PLANET THAT ACTUALLY MOVES

- **Plate tectonics** drift continents apart over millennia — *this is what splits one ancestral people into many races.*
- **Climate, seasons, ice ages** drive migration.
- **Rivers, plagues, volcanoes, meteors** reshape history.
- **One origin → divergence** via geography + adaptation + interbreeding.

Start on **Template Pangaea**, or plant a **Genesis Seed** and watch your continents be born. **Infinite time. No win condition. No ending.**

---

### SLIDE 8 — YOU, THE GOD

You **influence**; you rarely command. Powers cost **Faith** — earned when beings believe in you.

- **Inspire / Reveal / Whisper** — subtle nudges
- **Bless / Smite / Terraform** — overt acts of god
- **Possess** — be a being
- **Chronicle** — the auto-written history book of your world, replayable as cinema

Are you a loving gardener, an absent god, or a vengeful tyrant? **Your civilizations will theologize about you accordingly — and it shapes everything they become.**

---

### SLIDE 9 — LOOK & FEEL

> **GTA VI graphics. Sims 4 soul.**

- Photoreal-stylized: AAA material/lighting fidelity, but warm and readable so thousands of beings stay lovable.
- **Seamless scale**: orbital Pangaea → stubble on a being's face, no loads (UE5 Nanite/Lumen/Mass AI).
- Cities physically grow building-by-building across centuries.
- **Emergent conlangs** — each culture grows its own language and script.

---

### SLIDE 10 — WHY NOW

- **LLM agents** are finally fast and cheap enough (small models + caching) to give thousands of beings real minds.
- **UE5** delivers seamless planet-to-face fidelity out of the box.
- **The audience exists**: god-sim + emergent-narrative fandom (Rimworld, Dwarf Fortress, WorldBox, Civ, Sims) is huge and underserved by anything next-gen.

The pieces only just arrived. **First mover wins the category.**

---

### SLIDE 11 — BUSINESS MODEL

- **Premium base game** (the planet + sim).
- **Cosmetic/creative DLC** — biome packs, era aesthetics, god-avatar styles.
- **World Gallery** — publish your Pangaea, others fork & spectate (UGC engagement engine, viral loop).
- **Optional cloud sim** — your world keeps evolving while you're offline (subscription).
- *No pay-to-win — there's nothing to win. You monetize creativity and continuity.*

The emergent-story format is **inherently shareable** → organic streamer/TikTok growth (every world is a unique narrative).
